# Security policy

Please report security or privacy issues privately by email to **support@499apps.com** rather than in a public issue. Include the extension version, your browser version and steps to reproduce. We aim to reply within a few days and will credit you in the fix if you like.

Petit Mot runs entirely on your device: it makes no network requests and has no accounts or servers. Reports about anything that could leak page content or progress data are especially welcome.
